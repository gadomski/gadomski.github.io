Even if you're the principal violin in the New York Philharmonic, you can't create all of that music.
[Jalen Brunson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalen_Brunson) needs teammates.
Even in this age of agents, we need folks around us to help us.

In the _before times_, those of us who wrote software often had the ability to do things that others couldn't.
We could talk to the machines to create cool stuff.
That exclusivity has ended, and it is good that it has.
Technology only exists to elevate and enhance the human experience, so the more folks who can make it, the better.

When anyone can create software, how do we _shape_ and _build_ **systems** to elevate and enhance the human experience?
We need to **create an orchestra**.

What does this mean, in practice?

- Each individual who plays an instrument must be _excellent_ at playing that instrument
- We need many _instruments_, and the set of those instruments depends on the piece we're playing
- We need one or more people to coordinate those instruments, whether it is to pick the instruments that play or to pick how they play

Together, those instruments create music that affects people in deep, unimaginable ways.
